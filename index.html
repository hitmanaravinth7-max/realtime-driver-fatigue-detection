OpenCV (Computer Vision Role)

  • Video Frame Capture: Obtains continuous live video stream from vehicle cabin camera.
  • Image Preprocessing: Performs grayscale conversion and CLAHE (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization) to handle nighttime illumination.
  • Facial ROI Extraction: Utilizes Haar Cascade Classifiers / DNN face detector to localize face bounding box.
  • Geometric Metrics: Calculates Euclidean landmark distances for EAR and MAR formulas.
🧠

Keras & TensorFlow (Deep Learning Role)

  • Convolutional Architecture: MobileNetV2 with depthwise separable convolutions optimized for edge latency.
  • Texture Learning: Detects micro-closure textures, eyelid drooping patterns, and spectacles glare invariance.
  • Dataset Training: Fine-tuned on academic datasets including MRL Eye Dataset (37,000+ images) and YawDD.
  • Confidence Scoring: Computes softmax probability distributions to filter out driver speech vs biological yawning.

Mathematical Formulations (EAR & MAR)

1. Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) - Soukupová & Čech:
EAR = ( ||p2 - p6|| + ||p3 - p5|| ) / ( 2 * ||p1 - p4|| )

Where p1...p6 represent 2D eyelid landmark coordinates. EAR drops below 0.22 during eye closure. Sustained closure > 1.5s triggers micro-sleep alarm.

2. Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) - Yawn Geometric Metric:
MAR = ( ||m2 - m8|| + ||m3 - m7|| + ||m4 - m6|| ) / ( 2 * ||m1 - m5|| )

Where m1...m8 represent lip boundary landmarks. MAR exceeds 0.65 for sustained duration (>2.5s) during involuntary fatigue yawning.
